# 🤝 Contributing to Athan TV

First of all, thank you for your interest in Athan TV!

Athan TV is a free, community-driven project created to support Islamic education, daily reminders, families and mosques.

The project was initially developed by an enthusiast and beginner programmer with the assistance of AI tools, learning, experimentation and many hours of work.

It is now open to developers, designers, educators, imams and anyone willing to contribute positively.

## 🌍 Our vision

We want Athan TV to become a useful, educational and customizable companion for everyday Muslim life.

The project has two distinct environments:

- **Home Mode:** family education, Islamic reminders, widgets and customizable experiences.
- **Masjid Mode:** a respectful environment based on Adab al-Masjid and Adab al-Jumu'ah, with five Imam pages intended for content prepared or reviewed by qualified religious specialists.

Our goal is to preserve this distinction as the project evolves.

## 🛠️ How can you contribute?

You can help by:

- Fixing bugs and improving existing features.
- Improving Android TV and Google TV compatibility.
- Developing support for smartphones, tablets, computers and other platforms.
- Improving performance and application size.
- Improving the widget system and customization.
- Helping with translations and accessibility.
- Testing the application on different devices.
- Reviewing educational and religious content with qualified specialists.

## 🐛 Reporting bugs

Before opening an issue, please check whether the problem has already been reported.

When reporting a bug, include:

1. Your device and operating system.
2. The application version.
3. Steps to reproduce the problem.
4. What you expected to happen.
5. What actually happened.
6. Screenshots or logs, if available.

## 🔧 Submitting code

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your contribution.
3. Make focused changes.
4. Test your changes in the browser and, when relevant, on Android TV.
5. Submit a Pull Request explaining what you changed and why.

Please avoid unrelated modifications and do not include private credentials, API keys or personal information.

## 🕌 Important project principles

- Preserve the distinction between Home Mode and Masjid Mode.
- Respect the intended etiquette of mosques and Friday prayers.
- Religious content must be reviewed by qualified specialists.
- Do not remove existing features or media without discussion.
- Prefer improvements that preserve existing functionality.
- Be respectful and constructive in discussions.

## ❤️ A project offered for the benefit of the community

Athan TV is offered freely, with the intention of serving the community (Fi Sabili Allah).

There is no requirement to be an expert to contribute. Bug reports, testing, documentation, ideas and technical improvements are all valuable.

Thank you for helping this project grow.

**Athan TV — Technology for remembrance, education and everyday life.**
